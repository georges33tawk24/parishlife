Terms of use

Effective date: 22 November 2026

About these terms

ParishLife runs this service at parish-life.org. By using ParishLife you accept these terms. How we handle personal data is in the privacy policy.

Using ParishLife

  • Use it only for genuine parish purposes: your own parish life, or your role in a parish.
  • Give true information, and only about people you are entitled to act for.
  • Do not try to reach data your role does not allow, disrupt the service, get around the bot check or security controls, or use automated tools to collect data.
  • Do not post unlawful, abusive or misleading content, or other people's private information without their permission.

Accounts and security

  • You must be 13 or older to create an account; sign-up asks you to confirm it.
  • Keep your sign-in details private and tell us at once if you think someone else has used your account.
  • You are responsible for what is done with your account.
  • Parish staff, clergy and platform operators sign in with an authenticator app as a second step.
  • If you sign in with Google or Microsoft, their own terms apply to that account too.

Parish content

Records, documents and pages a parish creates belong to that parish, and the parish is responsible for them. What you send to a parish is shared with that parish so it can act on it.

Records and decisions

ParishLife helps parishes keep records and issue documents. Sacramental, canonical and other decisions remain with the parish and the Church authorities. Decisions taken outside ParishLife, or on information not confirmed with the parish, are not ParishLife's responsibility.

Payments and refunds

Using ParishLife as a member is free, and ParishLife takes no payments online, so there is nothing to refund through ParishLife. Gifts, registration fees, rentals and any refunds that a parish records are arranged directly with that parish, under its own rules; ask the parish office.

Reporting content

To report a message, use the report option on that message. To report other content that is unlawful, abusive or infringes your rights, write to privacy@parish-life.org.

Availability

We work to keep ParishLife available and the data safe, but we cannot promise that it will always be available or free of errors.

Suspension and closing

We may suspend an account, and a parish may end your access to its records, if the account breaks these terms or puts other people or the service at risk. You can stop using ParishLife at any time, and close your account yourself in My account ("Close my account") or by writing to privacy@parish-life.org. What closing deletes and what is kept is explained in the privacy policy.

Changes

We may change these terms. We will post the new text here with a new effective date.

Contact and law

Questions: privacy@parish-life.org. These terms are governed by the laws of Lebanon.
