## S10 — Import, duplicate review, archive and historical records

### S10.01

**Question:** Which records must be brought in before launch, from which real formats and with what approximate volume? Include paper registers and previous spreadsheets if applicable.

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:7109](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Inventory each parish's people/households, clergy/ministry rosters, historical sacramental entries, registrations, bookings, finance and documents before importing. Accept reviewed CSV/XLSX for structured data and approved scanned evidence; paper transcription is a separately reviewed historical-entry process.

Each import identifies parish, source owner/date, record type, field mapping and provenance. Actual file volumes/years must be supplied by the two offices; do not assume sample data represents production. Stage imports privately until validation and authorized reconciliation, and keep the original source securely for the agreed review/retention period.

**Reviewed completion:** Proposed first migration order: organizational identity/appointments and approved service templates; people/households and dated envelope links; active ministry/event/booking work; reconciled opening balances; reviewed historical official entries and evidence. Actual formats/volumes remain U11; XLSX support is a desired reviewed importer, not a claim of current code support.

**Owner / operational approval:** Office import owner; priest for official records; finance lead for balances. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Dependencies / detailed behavior:** [WF07](../workflow-specifications.md#wf07), [WF09](../workflow-specifications.md#wf09), [WF10](../workflow-specifications.md#wf10); [permission matrix](../permission-matrix.md); [section decision](../decision-register.md#s10). Scenario references: [A02](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a02), [A30](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a30).

**Acceptance:** Real source owners/formats/volumes are requested explicitly and historical transcription remains distinct.

### S10.04

**Question:** What identifies a likely duplicate, and which facts should reviewers compare before concluding two records represent the same person?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:7133](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Suggest duplicates using multiple permitted signals: supplied names in scripts, birth facts, parents/household, contact, locality and prior identifiers. Exact surname or shared family phone is never proof. Show why a candidate matched and where values differ, including archive/account-link status.

Reviewers compare appropriate evidence and choose Same person, Different people or Need information. Keep the match pending when uncertain; do not automatically merge during signup/import/request acceptance. Sensitive match evidence is available only to the necessary staff.
