## S18 — Ministries, leadership and membership applications

### S18.01

**Question:** Are Groups and Ministries interchangeable in your parish, or do you need types such as committee, catechism class, choir and temporary event team?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:7973](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Use a shared Ministries & Groups collection with types for ministry, choir, class/formation cohort, committee and temporary team. Type changes labels, eligibility and available tools without creating unrelated duplicate rosters.

Each group belongs to a parish, has responsible leaders/priests and clear visibility/status. Temporary teams can have end dates. Membership, attendance and staff leadership are separate facts. A public group listing contains only approved descriptions, not its internal roster or private files.

**Owner / operational approval:** Assigned ministry leader; parish priest; membership applicant. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Dependencies / detailed behavior:** [WF26](../workflow-specifications.md#wf26), [WF27](../workflow-specifications.md#wf27); [permission matrix](../permission-matrix.md); [section decision](../decision-register.md#s18). Scenario references: [A11](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a11), [A12](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a12).

**Acceptance:** Group type distinguishes ministry/choir/class/committee/team without changing record permissions by label.
