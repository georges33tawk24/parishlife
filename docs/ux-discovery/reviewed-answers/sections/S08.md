## S08 — People records and individual profiles

### S08.01

**Question:** Which categories of person belong in People: parishioners, visitors, children, nonmembers, non-Catholic spouses, clergy, deceased people and outside witnesses? Define their statuses.

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:6909](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** People may include members, applicants, visitors, children, clergy, nonmember spouses, guardians/witnesses and deceased/archived persons when operationally necessary. Separate relationship/category from lifecycle status: Active, Provisional, Deceased and Archived; approved membership is another fact.

Avoid a single Member status that falsely grants account access. External witnesses can remain limited reference entries rather than full community memberships. Each record belongs to a parish with provenance and permitted relationships. Deceased/archived status preserves official/financial links while affecting active communication and participation through reviewed rules.

**Owner / operational approval:** Head priest/office data owner; restricted-data policy owner. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Dependencies / detailed behavior:** [WF06](../workflow-specifications.md#wf06), [WF07](../workflow-specifications.md#wf07); [permission matrix](../permission-matrix.md); [section decision](../decision-register.md#s08). **Acceptance:** Person categories/lifecycle/membership remain separate and external witnesses do not become members automatically.

### S08.06

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Proposed answer:** Occupation/skills are optional for community participation and volunteering; show them only to authorized office/group users for that purpose.
