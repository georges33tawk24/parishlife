## S28 — Private notes, pastoral tasks and forms/workflows

### S28.01

**Question:** What belongs in personal member notes, confidential pastoral notes, group notes/tasks and office workflow tasks? Approve labels and boundaries that prevent accidental sharing.

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:9133](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Label account-private material **My notes**, author-owned clergy material **Private pastoral notes**, collaborative ministry material **Group notes/tasks**, and operational assignments **Office tasks/workflows**. Each shows its actual audience before saving.

A combined home is not a combined content store. Personal notes do not enter staff search; pastoral notes do not become People facts visible to every administrator. Group tasks use their group scope, while workflow answers use their own defined audience. Moving/sharing content requires explicit permission and preview, not a category change that silently publishes it.

**Owner / operational approval:** Note author; permitted task/workflow owner; privacy owner. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Acceptance:** Personal, pastoral, group and office task labels each show actual audience before saving.
