## S19 — Meetings, attendance, formation and group resources

### S19.01

**Question:** Does creating/editing a group meeting also create/update the calendar event, or should meetings and calendar events remain separate with an explicit link?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:8073](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** A meeting owns one linked calendar occurrence; creation/date changes update that occurrence through the meeting workflow. Keep agenda, roster, resources, RSVP and attendance attached to the meeting identity.

Calendar detail links back to the meeting and respects its edit rights. Recurring meetings have explicit occurrence/series actions, while completed attendance remains fixed to the actual occurrence. Do not create duplicate events from both screens. Public/group visibility and room/clergy conflicts are checked before publication/confirmation.

**Owner / operational approval:** Assigned leader; formation approver; authorized attendance recorder. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Dependencies / detailed behavior:** [WF28](../workflow-specifications.md#wf28), [WF29](../workflow-specifications.md#wf29), [WF65](../workflow-specifications.md#wf65); [permission matrix](../permission-matrix.md); [section decision](../decision-register.md#s19). Scenario references: [A11](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a11), [A12](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a12).

**Acceptance:** Group meeting owns one linked occurrence and date editing cannot produce conflicting independent calendars.

### S19.03

**Question:** Are RSVPs required, optional or inappropriate for some groups? Who can see absence reasons, and what is the deadline for changing a reply?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:8089](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** RSVP is configurable per meeting type and supports Yes, No and Undecided until a published deadline or meeting start. It is planning information, not recorded attendance. Absence reasons are optional and visible only to designated leaders/clergy who need them.

Members can update replies before the deadline; later changes use a leader contact/follow-up. Notify the organizer of meaningful changes without broadcasting private reasons. A missing RSVP is not No and must not automatically mark the person absent.

**Reviewed completion:** Default RSVP is optional for ordinary ministry meetings, required only when the published event/group policy actually needs planning counts. Deadline defaults to meeting start; coordinator may set earlier exact cutoff before publishing. Absence reason optional/private; RSVP No is not recorded absence.

### S19.04

**Question:** Which attendance states are needed: present, late, absent, excused, unknown and not expected? Should unrecorded attendance ever count as absence?

**Disposition:** Keep — preserve the explicit principle and apply the detailed rules below. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:8097](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Use Present, Late, Absent, Excused, Not expected and Not recorded. Preserve the distinction between unknown/unrecorded and actual absence. Configure whether Late counts toward a particular reporting measure and show that definition.

Default expected participants derive from the roster applicable on the meeting date, with reviewed guest/additional rows. A cancelled meeting has no ordinary absence denominator. Attendance entry requires an explicit meeting/date and actual recorder; a member RSVP or list membership cannot serve as proof of arrival.

**Reviewed completion:** Proposed metric uses Present + Late numerator; denominator is actual expected roster opportunities excluding Not expected, with Excused reported separately but retained in baseline denominator. Always display unrecorded count/coverage; a different approved group calculation must be versioned and labeled rather than silently changing old percentages.

**Owner / operational approval:** Assigned leader; formation approver; authorized attendance recorder. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.
