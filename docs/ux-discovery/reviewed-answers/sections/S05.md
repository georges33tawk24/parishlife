## S05 — Website content, announcements and publishing

### S05.01

**Question:** Who may edit public content, who may publish it, and should clergy or another reviewer approve particular types before publication?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:6609](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Parish Admins, secretaries and priests with content permission prepare public text/media within their parish. The head priest or an explicitly appointed public-content publisher approves ordinary publication; clergy titles, organizational facts and official sacramental wording require their competent authority. Leaders prepare material only for assigned ministries/audiences.

Keep edit and publish rights distinct, including urgent corrections. A colleague cannot approve their own content where a second review is configured. Every publication identifies editor, publisher, revision, language readiness and audience. Public access derives from approved publication, not from an internal record existing.

**Owner / operational approval:** Head priest/public-content publisher; EN/FR/AR reviewers. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Dependencies / detailed behavior:** [WF32](../workflow-specifications.md#wf32); [permission matrix](../permission-matrix.md); [section decision](../decision-register.md#s05). Scenario references: [A24](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a24).

**Acceptance:** Editor and publisher permissions are separate and domain facts still require their competent authority.

### S05.03

### S05.04

**Proposed answer:** Use Draft, Awaiting review, Returned, Approved/Scheduled, Published, Expired/Unpublished and Archived. ### S05.11
