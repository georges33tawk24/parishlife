## S07 — Following, parish membership and profile corrections

### S07.01

**Question:** What can a follower see/do before parish membership is approved, and what should explain the benefit and meaning of formal membership?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:6809](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Followers can read published parish events, schedules, ministry descriptions and announcements, contact the office and submit permitted guest-style requests from their account. Their combined home labels the relationship Following rather than Member.

Explain that approved parish membership links the account to a verified office person record and enables permitted personal/member features. It does not grant ministry leadership, finance or clergy access. Show membership request status and next action without blocking ordinary public browsing. Internal rosters, private group content and other people's household/sacramental records remain unavailable until independently authorized.

**Owner / operational approval:** Head priest/authorized office identity reviewer; applicant. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Dependencies / detailed behavior:** [WF04](../workflow-specifications.md#wf04), [WF05](../workflow-specifications.md#wf05); [permission matrix](../permission-matrix.md); [section decision](../decision-register.md#s07). Scenario references: [A01](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a01), [A02](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a02), [A04](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a04).

**Acceptance:** Follower gets published activity and request capability but no internal roster/person/staff access.

### S07.02
