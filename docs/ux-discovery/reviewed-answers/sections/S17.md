## S17 — Service/activity planning and music library

### S17.01

**Question:** What is a “service” versus a calendar event, sacrament request and ministry meeting in your users' vocabulary? Which should share one detail page?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:7873](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** A calendar event is the scheduled occurrence; a service/activity plan is its operational running order; a sacramental request is the clergy-approved case; a ministry meeting is group activity with roster/attendance.

Link them through a common event detail hub where appropriate, retaining distinct permissions/statuses. A plan cannot prove sacramental celebration or create membership. Display source/related records and route consequential date or approval edits to their owning workflow, avoiding duplicate calendars and contradictory names.

**Owner / operational approval:** Priest/plan coordinator; music librarian; rights reviewer. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Dependencies / detailed behavior:** [WF24](../workflow-specifications.md#wf24), [WF25](../workflow-specifications.md#wf25); [permission matrix](../permission-matrix.md); [section decision](../decision-register.md#s17).

**Acceptance:** Event occurrence, operational plan, sacramental case and meeting remain linked but distinct records.
