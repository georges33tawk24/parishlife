## S09 — Households, family relationships and representation

### S09.01

**Question:** What defines a household for your parish: shared residence, family relationship, shared finances or another rule? How should extended families at different addresses be represented?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:7009](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** A household is an office-managed current living/contact unit, not proof of legal guardianship, shared finances or automatic access to everyone's records. Extended family relationships can connect people across households without merging residences or permissions.

Store shared address/contact and optional offering-envelope association separately from kinship links. A family diagram represents recorded relationships rather than assuming a surname or address establishes parentage. Public/member access is limited to legitimately permitted details; an office household association is not a national family database visible to all parishes.

**Owner / operational approval:** Office data owner; authorized guardian/representation reviewer. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Dependencies / detailed behavior:** [WF08](../workflow-specifications.md#wf08); [permission matrix](../permission-matrix.md); [section decision](../decision-register.md#s09). Scenario references: [A20](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a20).

**Acceptance:** Household contact/residence grouping does not confer guardianship, shared finances or private-record access.
