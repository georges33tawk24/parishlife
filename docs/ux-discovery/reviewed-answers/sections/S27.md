## S27 — Concerns, complaints, reviewer independence and privacy

### S27.01

**Question:** What should the feature be called so users understand its purpose, and which matters belong here versus ordinary requests, feedback or incident reporting?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:9017](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Label the area **Concerns & complaints**, with plain explanations for confidential concerns, ordinary office requests, general suggestions and event incident reporting. Do not route a sensitive allegation through a public feedback form or broad office queue.

Provide a clear private submission path, expected reviewer access and maintained direct-help contacts where appropriate. A concern is not automatically a disciplinary finding. Case categories and language should be reviewed by the responsible safeguarding/independent authority, preserving supplied facts and avoiding accusatory default text.

**Owner / operational approval:** Independent authorized concern reviewers; conflict-free safeguarding authority. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

**Dependencies / detailed behavior:** [WF47](../workflow-specifications.md#wf47); [permission matrix](../permission-matrix.md); [section decision](../decision-register.md#s27). Scenario references: [A17](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a17), [A22](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a22).

**Acceptance:** Concerns & complaints scope differs from ordinary requests, feedback and incident reporting.
