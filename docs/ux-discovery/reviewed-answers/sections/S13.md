### S13.01

**Question:** For baptism, which child/adult, birth, parent, guardian, godparent, address and eligibility details are required at intake, preparation and official entry respectively?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:7441](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Intake captures subject's supplied names, birth date/place/precision, child/adult path, requester/relationship/contact, parents/guardian as known, parish and preferred date. Preparation adds verified identity/birth evidence, actual guardian authority, proposed godparents and the approved rite-specific eligibility/formation evidence.

The official entry adds actual celebration date/place, celebrant, approved parents/godparents/witness fields and register reference. Unknown/not applicable values are explicit and reviewed before official output, not fabricated. The bishop/priest supplies the real checklist and any required declarations; this specification defines data stages without inventing sacramental eligibility rules or demanding irrelevant fields from every family.

**Reviewed completion:** Minimum intake: requester/contact/authority, supplied child/adult name, known birth facts/precision and proposed parish/service. Preparation adds verified guardian/parent/godparent facts and domain-approved evidence. Official entry needs actual approved identity spellings, actual celebration/celebrant/place and mandatory jurisdiction fields. Unknown parent or script cannot block legitimate adult/provisional intake, while final required official facts remain genuine gates.

**Owner / operational approval:** Competent ecclesiastical authority and assigned parish priest. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.

Scenario references: [A03](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a03), [A06](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a06), [A30](../scenario-walkthroughs.md#a30).
