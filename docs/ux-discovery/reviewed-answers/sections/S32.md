### S32.01

**Question:** For people, accounts, official registers, certificates, requests, financial evidence, incidents, concerns, notes and messages, who approves retention rules and the actions available at expiry?

**Disposition:** Change — adopt the explicit behavior below, with new details proposed for review. **Release:** First release (retained module/design scope).

**Authority/evidence:** Proposed recommendation. Source: [docs/ux-discovery/complete-questionnaire-answers.md:9677](../../complete-questionnaire-answers.md). Canonical question: [questionnaire](../../questionnaire.md). New specification material is proposed unless explicitly identified as a recorded owner direction.

**Proposed answer:** Product/policy owners establish record-class retention with clergy authority for official records, finance owners for financial evidence and independent/safeguarding owners for incidents/concerns. Obtain applicable qualified policy review before adopting durations; none are invented here.

Define purpose, start event, period, legal/official hold, access after archive, permitted deletion/anonymization and backup treatment per class. Expiry creates reviewed disposition where required, not a blanket purge. Official entry deletion is not an ordinary user action. Private note/message policy remains distinct from institutional records.

**Reviewed completion:** For each record class, approve retention trigger, duration, custodian, permitted anonymization/deletion, holds and minimal disposal evidence. U08 identifies competent role owners; no default statutory time is made up. Interim no-purge/restricted access is temporary pending real policy, not indefinite retention approval.

**Owner / operational approval:** Product owners; records/privacy owner; ecclesiastical/finance/safeguarding approvers. Individual names have not been supplied; role ownership is not a fabricated personal sign-off. See [input register](../open-inputs-and-conflicts.md) for required evidence and milestones.
