## Worked fictional examples

**Giving and a recount:** Batch EX-COUNT-1 contains USD 100.00 cash and LBP 9,000,000 cash. Counter 2 independently observes USD 95.00 and LBP 9,000,000. Variance is USD 5.00; batch stays open. Recount identifies and documents the omitted USD 5.00, both reviewers confirm the new exact revision, and closure posts USD 100.00 plus LBP 9,000,000 once. A later USD 20.00 fund reclassification moves allocation from General to Repair, changing neither cash balance nor total received. Donor reassignment likewise changes attribution only; corrected statement references the original receipt.

**Event receipt allocated once:** Event registration costs USD 30.00 fee + USD 10.00 transport. Actual receipt TX-EX-1 is USD 25.00 and is allocated USD 20.00 to fee and USD 5.00 to transport. Outstanding = USD 15.00. Registration and Finance both reference TX-EX-1; sum of allocations = USD 25.00, not another cash transaction. On an approved full return of this receipt, refund TX-EX-2 is USD 25.00 and reverses exactly those allocations once. Cancelling the place without TX-EX-2 leaves a refundable credit, never a fictitious completed refund.

**Office allowance is internal transfer:** Parish bank USD 1,000.00 and office cash USD 0.00. Transfer TR-EX-1 sends USD 100.00 from bank to office; balances become bank USD 900.00 and office USD 100.00, total USD 1,000.00. The office pays USD 30.00 supplier purchase TX-EX-3; office cash USD 70.00, total USD 970.00. Parish-wide actual expense is USD 30.00, not USD 130.00. Source and destination transfer legs reference TR-EX-1; the actual purchase appears once, even if Finance displays an Office Expenses projection.

**Currency allocation:** An actual LBP 900,000 receipt is used toward a USD event balance using an explicitly fictional approved allocation rate of 90,000 LBP per USD, yielding USD 10.00 credit. Preserve actual LBP receipt and applied rate/date/source; do not record a second USD 10.00 cash receipt. Changing a reporting rate tomorrow must not rewrite that original allocation. USD rounding is half-up to cents; LBP whole-unit display/ledger precision is a proposed finance policy and requires approval.

**Capacity race:** Event has 10 places; 9 are reserved or confirmed. Two families submit concurrently. One atomic capacity action reserves the tenth place; the other is Waitlisted. A missing-consent reserved child still consumes a place but cannot be Confirmed/Checked in until actual age/consent policy is resolved. Payment receipt does not solve consent or imply entry permission.

## Source review and external references

Static source inspected for this pack: backend/guest_delivery.py release() confirms office context, selected attached file, identity-confirmation flag and issued/accepted-or-legacy certificate; public_file() currently checks token-hash/file/status. Proposed additional download-time identity gating is a design change, not a claimed fix. backend/workspace.py certificate-resubmission and bishop-review handlers retain returned/pending/accepted workflow; bundle handler at inspected lines 608–617 defines current module families and manual baseline. assets/auth.js explicitly reports unconfigured providers/verification/MFA. assets/persist.js uses server revision saves/recovery, not proof of offline authoritative writes. Relevant original source locations are listed in project-review.md. These are source observations; no runtime/backend parity tests were run for this documentation task.

The proposed new-password range is 16–200 characters, replacing the older 12-character ordinary-account proposal. NIST’s current digital-authentication guidance specifies at least 15 characters for a single-factor password and recommends allowing at least 64; ParishLife’s 16-character proposal is a product decision, not a claim that this parish must legally adopt a US government standard. Password-manager/paste support, compromised-password checks and real MFA/recovery need actual implementation/security review. [NIST SP 800-63B](https://pages.nist.gov/800-63-4/sp800-63b.html).

No source browsing establishes Lebanese legal retention, religious eligibility, certificate legal validity, finance/tax status or emergency procedure here. Those facts require competent jurisdiction-specific evidence under U02/U03/U05/U06/U07/U08.
