# Record/action permission matrix

Proposed complete action matrix. All rights require an active eligible appointment/grant in the exact organizational/record scope and the selected acting capacity; deny/case restrictions override a broad role. An omitted action is denied, not implicitly granted. “Office” means assigned priest, Parish Admin, secretary or explicitly authorized higher office administrator within scope; it never silently means a qualified signer, finance approver or independent concern reviewer.

For every row, **create/edit** includes stage-limited draft changes, **submit/review** includes returned information, **approve/proxy/sign** keeps these authorities distinct, **release/collect** checks actual recipient/authority, **export** includes print/download/search snippets, and **archive/restore/admin** cannot erase immutable history. Exact field restrictions resolve through the [field profiles](field-dictionary.md#privacy-editing-locking-and-output-profiles).

Immediate revocation applies on the next protected read/write/download and actual expiry, across open tabs. Unknown outcome after lost response is queried before retry. Removing one grant leaves unrelated valid grants and own-case tracking intact. Choosing another role does not create an independent second person.

## Reserved priest-instruction recording rule

D005 and supplied S02.06–S02.07 are preserved: the priest already made the case decision; the secretary records it under the secretary’s own identity, with named eligible priest, case/stage, instruction basis and actual entry timestamp. The decision is effective once recorded. Priest activity/notification is oversight, not another pending approval gate. New proposal: allow exactly case acceptance, verified official-entry approval and certificate-preparation approval, subject to competent clergy confirmation U04; never signature, bishop decision or document-release authority. An instruction entered after the alleged priest appointment ended requires historical review rather than current impersonated authority.

Printed official authority identifies the priest; internal history preserves both priest and entering secretary. A material signed-document edit requires new actual signature/review. Disputed instruction freezes uncompleted dependent work pending competent review, appends a correction and retains committed official history. Parish Admin preparation/office fallback does not inherit the secretary route unless a separate actual appointment/approved grant authorizes it.

## Financial viewer boundary

View/print/export are only within an explicit financial scope and permitted identity fields; they never allow entry, approve, pay, reverse, reconcile-close or mutate through old URLs. Public UI omission is backed by action checks. The no-edit result offers safe navigation and an access request without revealing other accounts or records.

## Authorizations still requiring people and policy

Actual appointer, priest, finance approver, independent concern authority, safeguarding event lead and language reviewer names are U01/U02/U06/U07/U14 inputs. This matrix names responsible roles, not fabricated personal approvals. Recorded owner policy governance by Super Admins/priests does not grant them content access to unrelated confidential records.
