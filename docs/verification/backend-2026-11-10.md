The detailed workflow there is a **proposed** recommendation, so role ownership is implemented without claiming a named clergy approval of any real program.

- Priest-approved, immutable program revisions with ordered prerequisite milestones and an optional clergy sign-off flag; revisions supersede the previous approved one and enrollments pin their exact revision.
- Enrollment of current, unarchived ministry members only, with an atomic per-person/revision claim.
- Milestone lifecycle Enrolled → InProgress → EvidenceSubmitted → Reviewed → Completed, with returned information/resubmission, priest reopening, enrollment withdrawal/reopening and append-only before/after history.
- New explicit leader duties `formation.record` and `formation.review` (permissions `ministries.formation.record` / `ministries.formation.review`). The evidence recorder cannot review or complete that evidence.
- Learner projection without evidence, provenance, review references, withdrawal reasons or staff identities. Completion is never derived from attendance and is labeled as not a sacramental certificate or eligibility decision.
